Kits

What is in each kit?

Every kit carries a CAT Tourniquet, QuikClot, HyFin chest seals, compressed gauze, a pressure dressing, ABD pads, gauze rolls, burn gel, a foil blanket, gloves, shears and a pen light. The bigger boxes carry more of each, and the 16" adds multi-trauma dressings. See every count

What is the difference between the 11" box and the IFAK?

The supplies are the same. The 11" Trauma Box, $216.58, is a sealed hard case for a boat console, truck or gear bag. The IFAK, $178.63, is a wearable kit for your belt, vest or bag.

Which kit should I buy?

Start with where it will live. A tight spot on a boat or in a truck: the 11". More room: the 13". Bigger boats, vehicles and team kits: the 16". On your body: the IFAK. A team, fleet or store: ask us for a quote.

Are the boxes waterproof?

Is the IFAK a hard case too?

No. The IFAK is a wearable kit made to stay on your belt, vest or bag. For a kit that lives on deck or in a truck, choose a hard-case box.

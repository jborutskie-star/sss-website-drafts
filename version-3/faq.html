Water and durability

Are the boxes waterproof?

The 11", 13" and 16" boxes are tough hard cases built to seal out water, salt, dust and impact, so your supplies stay dry and organized.

Is the IFAK a hard case too?

No. The IFAK is a wearable pouch. It carries the same supplies as the 11" Trauma Box.

Where can I keep a box?

Boat consoles, trucks, gear bags, range carts and gear areas. The 11" fits where larger cases will not.

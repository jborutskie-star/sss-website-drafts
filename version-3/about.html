We are active SWAT medics and fire captains.

More than 30 combined years in EMS and tactical medicine, based in Port St. Lucie, Florida.

We have spent our careers treating critical injuries in the field, from high-risk law enforcement operations to everyday emergency calls. We built our kits and training around what actually works when help is delayed and conditions are rough.

We design and sell practical, purpose-built trauma kits made for real environments: boats, trucks, ranges, hunting camps, and anywhere a standard first-aid kit falls short.

We also teach straightforward trauma and first-aid classes focused on the skills that matter most: controlling major bleeding, managing airway issues, and stabilizing someone until higher-level care arrives.

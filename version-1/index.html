Read this first.

When help is delayed and conditions are rough, a standard first-aid kit falls short. We build trauma kits for those places: boats, trucks, ranges and hunting camps.

Each one carries a CAT tourniquet, QuikClot and chest seals, packed so you can open, grab, and work. The boxes are sealed hard cases that keep out water, salt, dust and impact.

Built in Port St. Lucie by active SWAT medics and fire captains with more than 30 combined years in EMS and tactical medicine. Every kit and every class is built from the same standard we use on duty.

Short answers.

All questions and answers
Are the boxes waterproof?
The 11", 13" and 16" boxes are hard cases built to seal out water, salt, dust and impact.
Do you ship?
Yes. Kits are sold online and ship from Port St. Lucie, Florida.
Do you offer multi-kit or department discounts?
Yes. Departments, charters and dealers can request a quote for several kits.
What is the difference between the 11" box and the IFAK?
The supplies are the same. The 11" box is a sealed hard case for the boat or truck. The IFAK is a wearable kit that stays on you.

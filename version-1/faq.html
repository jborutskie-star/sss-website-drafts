Kits

What is in each kit?

Every kit carries a CAT Tourniquet, QuikClot, HyFin chest seals, compressed gauze, a pressure dressing, ABD pads, gauze rolls, burn gel, a foil blanket, gloves, shears and a pen light. The bigger boxes carry more of each, and the 16" adds multi-trauma dressings.

See each kit's packing list

What is the difference between the 11" box and the IFAK?

The supplies are the same. The 11" Trauma Box ($216.58) is a sealed hard case for a boat console, truck or gear bag. The IFAK ($178.63) is a wearable kit for your belt, vest or bag.

Which kit should I buy?

Start with where it will live. On the boat: the 11". In the truck: the 13". On your body: the IFAK. A team, fleet or store: ask us for a quote.

Water and durability

Are the boxes waterproof?

The 11", 13" and 16" boxes are tough hard cases built to seal out water, salt, dust and impact, so your supplies stay dry and organized. [Rating to confirm]

Is the IFAK a hard case too?

No. The IFAK is a wearable kit made to stay on your belt, vest or bag. For a kit that lives on deck or in a truck, choose a hard-case box.

Where can I keep a box?

Boat consoles, trucks, gear bags, range carts and gear areas. The 11" fits where larger cases will not.
